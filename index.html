Albums and groups

Browse folders in a grid or list, and gather albums into nested groups.

Learn more →
  • Every folder of photos or videos on your phone shows up as an album.
  • Tap + to create a group, then add albums to it. Groups can hold other groups too.
  • A group's tile shows a 2×2 preview of the albums inside.
  • Switch between large tiles, small tiles and a list.
↕️

Your order

Sort each view on its own, or drag items into exactly the order you want.

Learn more →
  • Sort by name, date or size, or pick Custom and long-press to drag items into place.
  • Each album and group can remember its own sort order and grid size.
  • Groups can stay pinned on top of your albums.
  • Changed your mind? Restore the custom order back to A–Z in one tap.
📁

Real file tools

Copy, move, rename and delete, with clear choices when names clash.

Learn more →
  • Select one item or many, then copy or move them to any album, or straight into a new one.
  • A progress window shows how far along it is, and you can cancel.
  • If a file with the same name already exists, choose Skip, Replace or Rename, and apply that choice to all of them.
  • Rename albums and delete items with a clear confirmation first.
🙈

Hide folders

Keep screenshots, downloads and other clutter out of sight.

Learn more →
  • Open the ⋮ menu and choose Hide album(s).
  • Switch off any folder you don't want to see in the gallery.
  • Your files aren't moved or deleted, only hidden from view. Unhide them any time.
🔌

USB drives

Open photos and videos stored on a connected USB drive.

Learn more →
  • Plug in a USB drive and pick it once in Android's file picker.
  • The app only gets access to the drive you choose.
  • Its albums appear in the gallery and come back automatically when you reconnect the drive.
💾

Backup and restore

Save your settings, groups and custom order to a file on your device.

Learn more →
  • A backup saves your settings, groups, hidden folders and custom order. Your photos and videos are not included.
  • Turn on Auto backup to keep it up to date as you make changes.
  • Moving to a new phone? Copy the backup file over and tap Restore.
