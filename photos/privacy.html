Solo Photos Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

In short: Solo Photos does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. Your images and settings never leave your device.

Who we are

Solo Photos ("the app") is developed by Pablo Martinez, an independent developer. For questions, contact sologallery.apps@outlook.com.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking. It does not send your images, file names or any information about you or your device to the developer or to anyone else.

How the app uses your images

Solo Photos reads the images on your device so it can show them to you. All processing, such as building thumbnails, sorting and searching, happens on your device. Albums, groups, hidden folders, custom order and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.

Permissions

PermissionWhy the app needs it
Photos and images (read media images)To find and show the images on your device.
All files accessTo copy, move, rename and delete images and albums anywhere on your storage when you ask it to, and to create new album folders. The app only acts on files when you take an action.
Manage mediaTo move images to the trash or change them without asking for confirmation each time, after you choose the action in the app.
Media locationThe app does not read, display or use location data stored in your photos. When the app copies or moves a photo, the file is kept exactly as it is.
USB drive accessOnly for a USB drive you choose in the system file picker, so the app can show images stored on it.

Backups

If you use Backup, the app writes a backup file to a location on your device that you can see. The file contains your app settings and groups, not your images. You decide whether to copy it anywhere else.

Third parties

The app does not include third-party advertising, analytics or tracking libraries, and shares no data with third parties.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

All app data stays on your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes it completely. Your images themselves are not deleted when you uninstall.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
