Frequently asked questions

Why are there two apps instead of one?

Each app focuses on one kind of media, so your photo gallery isn't mixed with videos, and the other way around. Install one or both.

Why does the app ask for "All files access"?

It lets the app copy, move, rename and delete items and create album folders anywhere on your storage, as a full gallery app can. The app only touches files when you take an action, and nothing is uploaded.

Can I use photos or videos on a USB drive?

Yes. Connect the drive, then choose it when the app asks. Android only gives the app access to the drive you pick.

How do I hide a folder?

Open Settings, then Hide folders, and turn off the folders you don't want to see. Hiding a folder only changes what the app shows. Your files are not moved or deleted.

How do I move my groups and order to a new phone?

Use Backup in Settings to save a backup file, copy it to the new phone, then use Restore. The backup holds settings, groups and custom order. Your media files are copied separately.

Does the app collect my data?

No. There are no accounts, ads or analytics, and nothing leaves your device. See the Solo Photos and Solo Videos privacy policies.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android Settings, then Apps. Your photos and videos are not deleted.
