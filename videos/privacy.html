Solo Videos Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

In short: Solo Videos does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. Your videos and settings never leave your device.

Who we are

Solo Videos ("the app") is developed by Pablo Martinez, an independent developer. For questions, contact sologallery.apps@outlook.com.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no tracking. It does not send your videos, file names or any information about you or your device to the developer or to anyone else.

How the app uses your videos

Solo Videos reads the videos on your device so it can show and play them. All processing, such as building thumbnails and previews, sorting and searching, happens on your device. Albums, groups, hidden folders, custom order and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device.

Permissions

PermissionWhy the app needs it
Videos (read media video)To find, show and play the videos on your device.
All files accessTo copy, move, rename and delete videos and albums anywhere on your storage when you ask it to, and to create new album folders. The app only acts on files when you take an action.
Manage mediaTo move videos to the trash or change them without asking for confirmation each time, after you choose the action in the app.
Media locationThe app does not read, display or use location data stored in your videos. When the app copies or moves a video, the file is kept exactly as it is.
Notifications and foreground serviceTo keep building video thumbnails and previews in the background, and to show you a progress notification while it does.
USB drive accessOnly for a USB drive you choose in the system file picker, so the app can show videos stored on it.

Backups

If you use Backup, the app writes a backup file to a location on your device that you can see. The file contains your app settings and groups, not your videos. You decide whether to copy it anywhere else.

Third parties

The app does not include third-party advertising, analytics or tracking libraries, and shares no data with third parties.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

All app data stays on your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes it completely. Your videos themselves are not deleted when you uninstall.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
